CrewCare

Privacy

CrewCare keeps your data on your own device.

What the app stores

Health check-ins you type in, messages you queue for Earth, and your chosen environment (ISS, Moon or Mars) are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only. There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking and no advertising.

What leaves the device

Only the messages you choose to send to Earth. Nothing else is sent anywhere. The app works with no internet connection at all.

Simulated data

The four crew members and their 30 days of readings are simulated demo data, not real people or real astronaut data. They are labelled simulated in the app.

Health data is not a diagnosis

CrewCare guides a non-doctor through checks and next steps. It does not diagnose anyone and does not replace flight-surgeon guidance.

Deleting your data

Clearing the browser's site data for this app removes everything it stored. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.
