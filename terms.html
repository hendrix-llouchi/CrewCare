CrewCare

Terms

CrewCare is a demonstration built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Takoradi.

What it is

An offline-first health companion for a spacecraft crew with no doctor on board. It shows health trends against each astronaut's own baseline, walks a non-doctor through emergency triage step by step, and queues messages to Earth.

What it is not

It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose anyone, does not name any disease, and does not replace flight-surgeon guidance. In a real emergency, follow your crew procedures and contact ground control.

Demo values

Crew data is simulated and labelled as such. Alert bands are team-defined demo values, except the eye (SANS) threshold of +20 micrometres early and +55 micrometres concerning, which is a published NASA research value. The Mars message delay is shortened to 30 seconds for the demo and labelled on screen; the real one-way delay is 20 to 24 minutes.

Sources

Design evidence comes from NASA's Human Research Program and the NASA Open Science Data Repository (studies OSD-816, OSD-1032, OSD-1040 and OSD-1023). Chart.js is used under its MIT licence; fonts are IBM Plex under the SIL Open Font License.

Use

Use the app as shown for the competition demo. Do not rely on it for real medical decisions.
